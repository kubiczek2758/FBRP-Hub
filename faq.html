Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining and playing on Fun Border Roleplay. Can't find your answer? Ask us on Discord.

How do I join an FBRP session?
Launch Emergency Response: Liberty County on Roblox, open the private server join box and enter the code RBPPP. Once you're in, follow the instructions during the server training session (STS), vote for your border and start roleplaying.
What is an STS?
STS (Server Training Session) is the briefing that starts every session. Staff go through the server rules, host a border vote and assign everyone to a border. Attending the STS is required before you can play.
How do I join a department?
Each department — FBI, U.S. Army, DOT, Homeland Security, Fire Department — has its own Discord server with its own application process. Visit the Departments section on the main site and join the Discord of the department you're interested in.
How do I become a staff member?
Fill out the Staff Application. If your application is accepted, you'll be invited to a training session with a Training Instructor — a classroom phase, a quiz and a practical ridealong.
When are staff promotions?
Promotions are held every Saturday. Sometimes there's a short delay — stay patient and keep up the good work.
How do I report a player who broke the rules?
Use the in-game mod call system first so on-duty staff can react immediately. If no staff are available, record evidence (video/screenshot) and open a report on our Discord — include the player's username, what happened and the evidence.
I was punished in-game. Can I appeal?
The in-game ban appeal system is coming soon. In the meantime, if you believe a punishment was a mistake, contact the staff member who issued it or reach out on Discord with your evidence.
What are the most important rules?
No RDM/VDM (killing without a roleplay reason), always value your life (FearRP), respect the New Life Rule, stay in character and never leave to avoid an arrest or punishment. Check the Rules section on the main site for the full list.
Where can I find the staff hierarchy?
The full rank structure — from Trial Moderator up to Ownership — is on the Chain of Command page. If you have a question or issue, always contact the lowest rank that can handle it before going higher.
Can I record or stream FBRP sessions?
Yes, content creation is welcome. A dedicated Media Application is coming soon — until then, make sure your content follows Roblox Terms of Service and the FBRP rules.
