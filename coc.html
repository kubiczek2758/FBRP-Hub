FBRP Hierarchy

Chain of Command

The Chain of Command establishes the hierarchical structure within FBRP, defining the flow of authority, responsibility and communication. It delineates reporting relationships, clarifies individual roles and duties, and ensures that staff members direct inquiries to the appropriate personnel rather than escalating every matter to ownership. A well-defined chain of command enhances accountability, minimizes ambiguity, and facilitates efficient coordination across the server.
