🚨 FBRP Staff Training Guide

⚠️ DISCLAIMER FOR INSTRUCTORS: If you are deemed incapable of effectively training Trial Moderators, you will be removed from the Training Instructor Program.

📋 Phase 0: Pre-Training Setup

Step 1: Trainee Notification & Preparation

1. Ping the Staff Trainee on Discord with the training invitation and private server code.
2. Head directly to the SD (Sheriff Department) Briefing Room to meet your trainee.

Step 2: Requirements Check

Before proceeding, verify that your trainee meets all requirements:

* Guns: Unequipped completely.
* Avatar: Realistic (no oversized accessories, memes, or non-RP gear).
* Equipment: Handcuffs and Taser equipped.
* Optional Voice Check: You may ask for an optional voice check.

🚨 CRITICAL NOTE: Voice checks are strictly optional. If your trainee chooses to speak and sounds under 13 years old, do not confront them directly—immediately flag an Internal Affairs member to investigate.

🚘 Phase 3: Practical Ridealong

Duration: ~20–30 Minutes

Location: Start at CPD Headquarters / District Station

Prep Checklist

1. Instruct the trainee to log into duty via the Melonly bot and equip their Staff Uniform.
2. Verify they have unequipped all weapons before spawning a Staff Vehicle.
3. Grant in-game Mod permissions only for the duration of the ridealong.

Procedure

1. Have the trainee drive around until a Mod Call appears.
2. Instruct them to pull over in a safe, non-disruptive location and click on the call.
3. Let the trainee handle the calls independently.

Field Rules for Instructors

* 🛑 Hands Off: Do not step in to help your trainee unless it is absolutely necessary (e.g., severe troll/exploit scenario).
* 💡 Coaching: You are allowed to give tips if your trainee gets a little stuck, but do not handle the scene for them.
* ✍️ SPaG Enforcement: Remind your trainee to use proper SPaG if they are not doing so.
* 🔒 Permissions: Only mod your trainee while you are with them. If they do not pass, ensure you remove permissions before they leave.


🏁 Phase 4: Dismissal & Conclusion

1. Bring the trainee back to the CPD Briefing Room.
2. Discuss their results and ridealong performance (highlight strengths and areas to improve).
3. Answer any final questions before dismissing them.
4. Remind them to clock off duty if they are not staying on for an active shift.
5. Revoke Mod permissions (if not keeping them on duty) and log the completed training in channel.
