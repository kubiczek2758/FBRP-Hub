Server Rules

Rules of the Border

The full punishment list with exact consequences is available to staff on the Staff Site. These are the rules every player must follow in every session.

General Conduct

How you behave on and off duty.

1

Respect everyone. No toxicity, harassment, slurs or insults towards players or staff (DISS).

2

Follow Roblox Terms of Service at all times — breaking them results in a ban and report.

3

No exploiting, glitching or alt accounts. Using exploits or evading punishments on an alt leads to a ban.

4

Listen to staff. Staff instructions are final. Arguing, lying to staff (LTS) or evading staff actions (SEV) is punished.

Roleplay Standards

What we expect from your roleplay.

5

Stay in character. Joining without intent to roleplay (NITRP) or ruining scenes for others is prohibited.

6

Value your life. Under gunpoint or in danger, react realistically — your character fears for their life (FearRP).

7

New Life Rule. After death or respawn, forget your previous life — no returning to the same scene (NLR).

8

No RDM or VDM. Killing or hitting anyone without a valid roleplay reason is punished, and staff are never a target (SRDM/SVDM).

Driving & Vehicles

Keep it realistic behind the wheel.

9

Drive realistically. No GTA-style driving, flying through intersections or crashing without roleplay reason (GTAD).

10

Use correct liveries. Department vehicles require the proper uniform and livery — no random cruisers (NLU/NWL).

11

No rush tactics. Cuff rushing (CURU) and tow rushing (TR) without proper roleplay are against the rules.

Arrests & Punishments

Don't run from consequences.

12

Don't avoid arrests. Respawning or leaving to escape arrest (RTAA/LTAA) counts as combat logging.

13

Don't avoid punishments. Leaving during a staff scene or right after a kick (LTAP/LTAMS/JBAK) leads straight to a ban.

14

Keep your character realistic. Unrealistic avatars must be changed when staff ask (UA).
