1. STS Preparation

Use :h for important server-wide announcements before the STS.

15–10 minutes before:

:h FBRP STS preparations are now underway. Please finish your current RP and prepare for the upcoming STS. :h FBRP STS will begin shortly. Please make your way to the designated STS location. :h Staff members, please prepare for the upcoming STS and report to the STS location.

5 minutes before:

:h FBRP STS will begin in approximately 5 minutes. Please line up and wait for further instructions. :h Final preparations for the FBRP STS are now underway. Please remain at the STS area and follow staff instructions.

2. Starting the STS

Once everyone is ready:

:h FBRP STS is now beginning. Please remain in formation and follow the host's instructions. :m Welcome to the FBRP STS. Please remain in your position, face the front, and listen to the instructor. :m Do not move, talk over the instructor, use weapons, or disrupt the STS.

3. Formation

:m Please form a straight line with reasonable spacing between each person. :m Keep your position and do not push or walk through the formation. :m Staff will correct the formation. Please follow their instructions immediately.

4. During the STS

Use :m for the actual instructions and rules.

:m We will now begin going through the FBRP rules. Please pay attention. :m RDM — Random Deathmatch. Killing or attacking another player without a valid RP reason is prohibited. :m VDM — Vehicle Deathmatch. Intentionally hitting or running over another player without a valid RP reason is prohibited. :m FRP — Fail Roleplay. Players must keep their actions realistic and appropriate for the situation. :m NITRP — No Intent to Roleplay. Players must actively participate in RP and should not intentionally ruin situations. :m FearRP — Players must value their character's life and react realistically to dangerous situations. :m Combat Logging — Leaving the server to avoid an active RP situation, arrest, or punishment is prohibited.

5. STS Voting

:m Voting will now begin! React with the corresponding emoji for the border you would like to attend: Stand — High Rock Border | Sit — Highway Border | Springfield Roleplay — Lay down | River City Roleplay — Hands Up :m Please react with only one option. :m Voting is now closed. Thank you for voting! :m The border assignments will now be announced. Please wait for further instructions from Staff.

6. Keeping Order

If people start disrupting the STS:

:h Please remain quiet while the instructor is speaking. Continued disruption may result in removal from the STS. :h Stop moving around and return to your assigned position. :h This is your final reminder to follow STS instructions.

If necessary:

:m STS is temporarily paused due to disruption. Please remain in your positions and wait for further instructions.

7. Ending the STS

:m The FBRP STS is now coming to an end. Please remain in formation until dismissed. :m Thank you for attending the FBRP STS. We appreciate your cooperation. :h FBRP STS has now concluded. Thank you to everyone who participated. :m You are now dismissed. Have a great roleplay!
Command usage: :h for major announcements, preparation, and start/end of the STS. :m for instructions, rules, scenarios and general STS messages. Teleport/moderation commands — only when necessary. Do not spam :h; keep important announcements limited so players actually pay attention.
Disclaimer for instructors: If you are deemed incapable of effectively training Trial Moderators, you will be removed from the Training Instructor Program.

Phase 0: Pre-Training Setup

Step 1 — Trainee notification & preparation

  • Ping the Staff Trainee on Discord with the training invitation and private server code.
  • Head directly to the SD (Sheriff Department) Briefing Room to meet your trainee.

Step 2 — Requirements check

  • Guns: unequipped completely.
  • Avatar: realistic (no oversized accessories, memes, or non-RP gear).
  • Equipment: handcuffs and taser equipped.
  • Optional voice check: you may ask for an optional voice check.
Critical note: Voice checks are strictly optional. If your trainee chooses to speak and sounds under 13 years old, do not confront them directly — immediately flag an Internal Affairs member to investigate.

Phase 1: Classroom Phase

Copy and paste the highlighted lines into the in-game chat bar. Text in brackets [like this] is instruction only — never paste it into chat.

1. Welcome & Introduction

:m Welcome to the Classroom Phase! I am your Training Instructor, [Your Username], and I will be guiding you today. :m During this phase, we will cover the core guidelines and information required while working here at FBRP! :m Following the lesson, you will undergo a quiz. There will be 10 questions and you are required to answer each of them to pass. :m Are you ready before we continue with the training?

2. Core Curriculum (Lecture) — advise the trainee to take notes; they type "done" in chat when ready for the next section.

:m Chain of Command: The FBRP Chain of Command consists of [TO DO, SOON NEW] :m Core Values: As a staff member here at FBRP, you must uphold our 7 core values: Integrity, Respect, Determination, Excellence, Honesty, Diligence, and Professionalism. :m Infractions: Depending on severity, infractions include: Warning, Strike, Suspension, Demotion, Re-training, Termination, Blacklist, or Unappealable Staff Blacklist. :m Escalation Scale: Punishments follow a 3-strikes system (e.g., 3 Warnings = 1 Strike | 3 Strikes = 1 Suspension | 3 Suspensions = 1 Demotion | 3 Demotions = 1 Termination | 3 Terminations = 1 Blacklist | 3 Blacklists = 1 Unappealable Staff Blacklist). :m Re-trainings: Re-trainings are given on special occasions at the sole discretion of the Community Directors instead of a termination. :m Promotions: Promotions happen on Saturday each week. Sometimes there is a delay, so you must remain patient. :m On-Duty Expectations: When you go on duty, start your shift using /shift manage. You are required to use proper SPaG (Spelling, Punctuation, and Grammar) at all times. :m Logging Punishments: Log punishments via the Melonly bot using /logs create (Include User, Punishment, and Reason). NEVER log a ban unless instructed by someone with ban permissions, and sign their name on the log. Alternatively, use the Melonly Website or type :log in-game. :m Leave of Absence (LOA): All LOA requests must be submitted through the Melonly bot.

3. Comprehension check

:m Have you understood everything that I have stated? :m We will now start the quiz, which will cover what we have said as well as basic moderation questions. If you have any final questions, ask them now.

Phase 3: Practical Ridealong (~20–30 min)

Location: Start at CPD Headquarters / District Station.

Prep checklist

  • Instruct the trainee to log into duty via the Melonly bot and equip their Staff Uniform.
  • Verify they have unequipped all weapons before spawning a Staff Vehicle.
  • Grant in-game Mod permissions only for the duration of the ridealong.

Procedure

  • Have the trainee drive around until a Mod Call appears.
  • Instruct them to pull over in a safe, non-disruptive location and click on the call.
  • Let the trainee handle the calls independently.

Field rules for instructors

  • Hands off: do not step in unless absolutely necessary (e.g., severe troll/exploit scenario).
  • Coaching: give tips if the trainee gets stuck, but do not handle the scene for them.
  • SPaG enforcement: remind them to use proper SPaG if they are not doing so.
  • Permissions: only mod your trainee while you are with them. If they do not pass, remove permissions before they leave.

Phase 4: Dismissal & Conclusion

  • Bring the trainee back to the CPD Briefing Room.
  • Discuss their results and ridealong performance (strengths and areas to improve).
  • Answer any final questions before dismissing them.
  • Remind them to clock off duty if they are not staying on for an active shift.
  • Revoke Mod permissions (if not keeping them on duty) and log the completed training in channel.
