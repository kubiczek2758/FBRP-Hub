FBRP | STS Guide
1. STS Preparation
Use :h for important server-wide announcements before the STS.
15–10 minutes before:
:h FBRP STS preparations are now underway. Please finish your current RP and prepare for the upcoming STS.
:h FBRP STS will begin shortly. Please make your way to the designated STS location.
:h Staff members, please prepare for the upcoming STS and report to the STS location.
5 minutes before:
:h FBRP STS will begin in approximately 5 minutes. Please line up and wait for further instructions.
:h Final preparations for the FBRP STS are now underway. Please remain at the STS area and follow staff instructions.
2. Starting the STS
Once everyone is ready:
:h FBRP STS is now beginning. Please remain in formation and follow the host's instructions.
:m Welcome to the FBRP STS. Please remain in your position, face the front, and listen to the instructor.
:m Do not move, talk over the instructor, use weapons, or disrupt the STS.

3. Formation
:m Please form a straight line with reasonable spacing between each person.
:m Keep your position and do not push or walk through the formation.
:m Staff will correct the formation. Please follow their instructions immediately.
4. During the STS
Use :m for the actual instructions and rules.
:m We will now begin going through the FBRP rules. Please pay attention.
:m RDM — Random Deathmatch. Killing or attacking another player without a valid RP reason is prohibited.
:m VDM — Vehicle Deathmatch. Intentionally hitting or running over another player without a valid RP reason is prohibited.
:m FRP — Fail Roleplay. Players must keep their actions realistic and appropriate for the situation.
:m NITRP — No Intent to Roleplay. Players must actively participate in RP and should not intentionally ruin situations.
:m FearRP — Players must value their character's life and react realistically to dangerous situations.
:m Combat Logging — Leaving the server to avoid an active RP situation, arrest, or punishment is prohibited.
5. STS Voting
:m Voting will now begin! React with the corresponding emoji for the border you would like to attend: Stand — High Rock Border | Sit — Highway Border | Springfield Roleplay - Lay down | River City Roleplay - Hands Up

:m Please react with only one option.
:m Voting is now closed. Thank you for voting!
:m The border assignments will now be announced. Please wait for further instructions from Staff.
6. Keeping Order
If people start disrupting the STS:
:h Please remain quiet while the instructor is speaking. Continued disruption may result in removal from the STS.
:h Stop moving around and return to your assigned position.
:h This is your final reminder to follow STS instructions.
If necessary:
:m STS is temporarily paused due to disruption. Please remain in your positions and wait for further instructions.
7. Ending the STS
:m The FBRP STS is now coming to an end. Please remain in formation until dismissed.
:m Thank you for attending the FBRP STS. We appreciate your cooperation.
:h FBRP STS has now concluded. Thank you to everyone who participated.
:m You are now dismissed. Have a great roleplay!
Command Usage
:h — Major announcements / preparation / start and end of STS.
:m — Instructions, rules, scenarios and general STS messages.
Teleport / moderation commands — Only use when necessary to organize or control the STS.
Do not spam :h. Keep important announcements limited so players actually pay attention to them.
